PSAK and ISAK Number Conversion Table: From PSAK 1 to PSAK 201, PSAK 24 to PSAK 219

How to read the three-digit SAK Indonesia numbers and a complete list of old to new numbers

By Hendrawanto, SE., Ak., CA., CLI., AseanCPA., CPA., CFI. · 12 November 2026 · Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia

Many readers still search for "PSAK 24", "PSAK 2" or "PSAK 73", while financial statements, audit working papers and professional exams now use PSAK 219, PSAK 207 and PSAK 116. From 1 January 2024, the Financial Accounting Standards Board of the Institute of Indonesia Chartered Accountants (DSAK IAI) applied a new numbering system to PSAK and ISAK in Indonesian Financial Accounting Standards (SAK Indonesia). This article explains how to read the new numbers and provides a complete conversion table from the old numbers to the new ones.

The most important point first: the change only replaces the numbers. The requirements of each standard did not change because of the renumbering.

How to Read the Three-Digit Numbers

Every PSAK and ISAK number now has three digits.

  • The first digit shows the source the standard refers to.
  • The second and third digits follow the number of the referenced standard.
Prefix PSAK refers to ISAK refers to Example
1xx IFRS Accounting Standards IFRIC Interpretations PSAK 116 Leases (IFRS 16)
2xx IAS SIC Interpretations PSAK 219 Employee Benefits (IAS 19)
3xx Local standards, not referring to IFRS Local interpretations PSAK 338 Business Combinations of Entities under Common Control
4xx Sharia standards (SAK Syariah) Sharia interpretations PSAK 402 Murabahah Accounting

With this pattern, the new number also tells you the international equivalent. PSAK 207 Statement of Cash Flows, for example, reads straight away as the equivalent of IAS 7. PSAK 109 Financial Instruments is the equivalent of IFRS 9.

Note the trap in this table. Old numbers PSAK 101 to PSAK 112 are sharia standards, while new numbers PSAK 102 to PSAK 117 are IFRS-based standards. "PSAK 109" in an older document means Zakat Accounting, but in a newer document it means Financial Instruments. Always check the date of the document before interpreting a number.

Standards Issued after the Renumbering

Some standards issued after 2024 use the new numbers from the start and have no old number. Examples are PSAK 118 Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements, which will replace PSAK 201; PSAK 119 Subsidiaries without Public Accountability: Disclosures; and PSAK 120 Regulatory Assets and Regulatory Liabilities. They are discussed in the articles on PSAK 118, PSAK 119, and PSAK 120.

What It Means for Preparers, Auditors and Exam Candidates

  • Financial statements and notes should use the new numbers consistently, including when describing accounting policies.
  • Audit working papers and representation letters should be updated so that their references match the audited financial statements.
  • Older documents such as loan agreements and internal policies that still cite old numbers are not automatically wrong. They still refer to the same standards, but their next revision should use the new numbers.
  • Professional exam candidates should memorise the new numbers. Current questions and study materials use three-digit numbers.

Conclusion

The new numbering makes SAK Indonesia easier to map to IFRS. Prefixes 1 and 2 show the IFRS and IAS equivalents, prefix 3 shows local standards, and prefix 4 shows sharia standards. Keep the tables above as a quick reference, and always check the date of a document so that old and new numbers are not confused.
