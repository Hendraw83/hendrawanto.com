Following Up on BPK Recommendations: Process and Boundaries

By Hendrawanto · October 16, 2026

Follow-up on audit recommendations is a practical governance process. It should identify the accountable owner, the action, its evidence, the target date and a method to assess effectiveness.

Clear evidence and monitoring

A policy document may show that a rule was issued, but further records may be necessary to demonstrate that it is operating. A recommendation register, periodic monitoring and documented decisions help organisations keep a reliable trail of action.

Governance and legal processes

Administrative improvement, recovery and legal action can involve different authorities. Internal corrective action should not be treated as a conclusion about individual responsibility, and each matter must follow the applicable rules.

Conclusion

Recommendations are best handled as a sustained accountability process, with relevant evidence and careful monitoring.
