Expert Says the Interpretation of State Loss Is Still Too Broad

Criminal law expert Chairul Huda responded to the National Economic Council's proposal to rework the element of state financial loss. Banten RayaState Loss23 October 2026

State Loss Is Not a Guessing Figure (Kerugian Negara Bukan Angka Tebakan)

An opinion piece by Hendrawanto in Banten Raya's Opinion section, responding to the debate over the National Economic Council (DEN) proposal and its clarification that state financial loss remains an element of the Anti-Corruption Law. The author argues that the main issue is the reliability of loss figures, which often shape public perception before any verdict. The piece outlines the existing safeguards: the definition of state loss in the State Treasury Law, Constitutional Court Decision 25/PUU-XIV/2016 (actual loss, not potential loss), and Constitutional Court Decision 66/PUU-XXIV/2026. It also identifies three recurring errors in loss calculations and recommends that auditors separate administrative noncompliance, actual loss, potential loss, and nonfinancial impact, and disclose the methods they use.
