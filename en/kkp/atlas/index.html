Every paper is linked: enter the trial balance once and materiality, account-level risks, lead schedules, adjustments, the opinion and the draft report are calculated for you.

What is included

A.1 · PRE-ENGAGEMENT

A110 – A170

Client acceptance and continuance with automatic risk rating, prior reporting issues, management integrity, AML/CFT customer due diligence, team competence and independence, budgeted hours, engagement letter, assignment letter, independence declarations, team communication, and opening balances for first-year audits.

A.2 · RISK ASSESSMENT

A210 – A280

Materiality (overall, performance, clearly trivial, specific), preliminary analytics and ratios, understanding the entity A230.1–A230.8 including fraud risk factors, inherent risk by line item, entity-level (COSO) and process-level controls, communication with those charged with governance and internal audit, RoMM, and the audit strategy memorandum.

B · RISK RESPONSE

B110 – B280

Working trial balance, lead schedules with sample size and the ATLAS procedure bank, audit adjustments and uncorrected misstatements, audited financial statements, accounting estimates, related parties, subsequent events, going concern (Altman Z-score), representations, experts, and commitments and contingencies.

C · COMPLETION

C110 – C530

Final materiality, final analytical review, disclosure review, engagement quality review, evaluation of evidence with the suggested opinion (SA 700/705), report review, audit completion memorandum, and the draft independent auditor's report.

Improvements over the ATLAS Excel workbook

SA 315

Significant risk factors

Significant risks are derived automatically from the six SA 315 considerations together with likelihood and magnitude; RoMM = IR × CR.

SA 240

Presumed fraud risks

Revenue recognition and management override of controls, with journal entry testing procedures added to the lead schedules.

COSO 2013

Entity-level controls

Five components and 17 principles of internal control plus IT general controls and a deficiency log (SA 265).

PSAK 2024 · SAK EP

Current disclosure review

Checklist follows the renumbered PSAK (201, 207, 115, 116, 219 and more), SAK EP effective 2025, SAK EMKM and public sector standards.

SA 700 (Revised 2021)

Automatic draft report

Latest report structure including material uncertainty related to going concern, key audit matters (SA 701), emphasis of matter and other matter.

SPM · SA 230

Review trail and status

Preparer/reviewer sign-off with dates and hours on every paper; Completed/Incompleted status is calculated automatically and actual hours flow into A120.

How it works

  1. Complete the home page and the engagement team (A1103).
  2. Paste the trial balance from Excel into D100 – line items, groups and ratio tags are suggested automatically.
  3. Work through A.1 and A.2: materiality, analytics, inherent risk, controls and RoMM are calculated for you.
  4. Complete the B120 lead schedules for material or risky items and record adjustments in B130.
  5. Finish section C: evaluation of evidence, the suggested opinion and the draft report.
  6. Click Simpan Berkas (Save File) to keep your work and Unduh KKP (Download) for a PDF of all working papers.

ATLAS (Audit Tool and Linked Archive System) is an Excel application developed by the Indonesian Institute of Certified Public Accountants (IAPI) with the Finance Profession Supervisory Centre (PPPK) of the Ministry of Finance. ATLAS Online is an independent development by Hendrawanto that follows the ATLAS index structure and is updated to current Indonesian Standards on Auditing, SAK and regulations; it is not an official IAPI or PPPK product and does not replace the standards or the auditor’s professional judgement.
