What is included

B.1.1-WP

Work program

12 substantive procedures with assertions, status and disclosure requirements.

B.1.1-Lead

Lead schedule

Book balance, adjustments, audited balance, prior-year comparison and disclosure classification.

B.1.1-1A

Cash count sheet

Denominations, vouchers held by the custodian, difference, statement and signatures.

B.1.1-1

Cash roll-back

The counted balance is rolled back to the balance sheet date automatically.

B.1.1-2

Bank confirmations

Request letter per bank, reply form and confirmation control.

Cut-off tests

Disbursements (outstanding cheques) and receipts (deposits in transit).

B.1.1-5

FX restatement

Valuation of foreign-currency balances at the Bank Indonesia middle rate.

B.1.1-AJE

Proposed adjustments

Adjusting entries that flow into the lead schedule automatically.
