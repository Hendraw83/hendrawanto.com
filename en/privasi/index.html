WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy & Visit Statistics

Effective 5 October 2026. This information explains visit statistics and communications through hendrawanto.com.

Visit statistics

With visitor permission, public pages use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, sessions, viewed pages, and approximate country/city locations. Google may process technical information such as IP addresses, devices, browsers, and analytics cookie identifiers. Location information does not identify an exact address or individual.

Public visit counter

The homepage displays an aggregate visit counter starting on 5 October 2026. It uses Hits, separately from Google Analytics, only after Allow statistics is selected. A new visit is counted after at least 30 minutes of inactivity in the same browser. A timestamp is stored locally for this purpose; no name, form contents, query string, or location is sent to the counter. The request hides the referrer. The provider states that its badge counter does not store IP addresses, cookies, or user agent strings; the network request still reaches its servers. The count is not a unique-person count or a count of people currently online. It excludes operational apps and calculator demos; blocked storage, bots, blockers, and direct counter requests may affect the result. The public total is read from a saved aggregate on GitHub, refreshed approximately every 30 minutes; schedules and caches can delay updates. The displayed time identifies the saved count. Hits privacy policy.

Visitor choices & cookies

The Google tag is not loaded before you choose Allow statistics. Declining still allows you to use the website. Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days; statistics cookies have a maximum lifetime of 180 days. Analytics server retention follows the property settings controlled by the website owner. You can change your choice through Statistics preferences in the footer.

Collection boundaries

The Analytics loader is not installed on client forms, office apps, or calculator demos. The page URL configured for statistics excludes query strings and fragments, and the referrer is limited to its origin. Further collection is paused when you enter/submit a form or open a link with message/personal parameters. The website analytics code does not send form contents. Google advertising features are disabled.

Communication & confidentiality

The homepage contact form uses your entries on your device to prepare a WhatsApp message. A message is sent after you choose to send it in WhatsApp. Client forms and operational apps have separate workflows. Use agreed channels for financial documents, other people’s personal data, and confidential information.

Third-party services

Hosting, Google Analytics, the YouTube player, WhatsApp, and external links may process data under their respective policies. Declining analytics does not block video players or other services. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Analytics data safeguards.

Questions

For questions about communications or website use, contact info@hendrawanto.com. Statistics are aggregate reports; they exclude visitors who decline/block analytics and do not represent an exact count of all people.
